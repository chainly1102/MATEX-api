About Our Organization

Founded in Taichung, Taiwan, MATEXS CO., LTD. possesses 30 years of experience in traditional manufacturing. We operate as an Amazon seller, having transitioned from a traditional manufacturer into an e-commerce trading company. We have a established operational foundation and possess internal automation and business intelligence tools. We apply our manufacturing expertise directly to supply chain management and product quality monitoring. We utilize the SP-API to integrate our existing internal systems with Amazon's marketplace to synchronize operational data and manage our business efficiency.

Privacy Policy

  • Data Minimization: We only request and process the minimum public marketplace and order metrics necessary for our internal inventory planning.
  • Encryption at Rest: All data retrieved via Amazon SP-API is securely stored on our dedicated internal Windows Server infrastructure, which is fully encrypted utilizing Windows BitLocker Drive Encryption with AES-256 XTS standards.
  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between our internal automated nodes (n8n) and Amazon SP-API endpoints are strictly enforced via HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Data Retention & Deletion: All operational and non-historical marketplace data fetched from SP-API will be automatically purged within 30 days using sequential database sanitization processes in compliance with NIST 800-88 standards. Our Windows Server access management enforces password history for the last 10 passwords and account lockout after 10 failed attempts.
  • Zero Third-Party Sharing: We do NOT sell, lease, or share any Amazon marketplace or seller data with any external third parties or software vendors.
